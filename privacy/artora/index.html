Privacy Policy — Artora

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Overview

Artora ("the App") is an iOS application that turns your photos into anime art, pencil-style line-art sketches, and 3D motion MP4 videos using on-device AI. All image and video processing happens entirely on your device. No photos or videos are uploaded to any server.

3D Motion Videos

The 3D Motion tool estimates relative image depth on your device. You can rotate the preview, record its camera movement, replay the recording and export an MP4 video. Your selected photo, generated depth map, recorded camera movement and exported video are processed locally and are not sent to the developer or a third-party processing service. The tool does not record from your device's camera or microphone.

Depth is an artistic relative-depth estimate, not a measurement or a reconstruction of hidden parts of a scene.

Data Collection

The developer does not receive or collect your photos, videos or personal data. Artora processes your selected content locally on your device.
  • All image processing is performed 100% offline using on-device AI models and native image APIs.
  • No user accounts are required.
  • No photos are uploaded to any server.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included. There are no ads.

Permissions

The App requests only the minimum access necessary:

  • Photo selection: Artora receives only the photo you explicitly select through the system photo picker. Selecting a photo does not require access to your entire photo library.
  • Photo Library (add): Permission is requested to save your generated artwork or MP4 video to your photo library.

The App does not request camera, location, contacts, or any other sensitive permissions.

Purchases

Artora offers an optional one-time in-app purchase ("Artora Pro") that removes the watermark from artwork and 3D motion MP4 exports and unlocks all art styles. Free 3D Motion exports include a watermark; Pro exports can be saved without it. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. The developer does not receive or store your payment information. Artora checks purchase entitlements through Apple's StoreKit APIs, and purchase or restore operations may communicate with Apple.

Data Storage

  • Selected photos, generated depth maps and recorded camera movements are held locally while you use the tool.
  • MP4 export creates a temporary file on your device. Artora attempts to delete that file after saving, failure or cancellation. An unexpected app termination may leave a temporary file until it is removed by the app or operating system.
  • Generated artwork and MP4 videos are saved to your device's photo library only when you choose to save and grant the required permission.
  • Original photos are never modified in place.
  • All AI models are bundled with the App and do not download additional data.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its local App-specific data. Saved images and videos remain in your photo library until you delete them. Your device's Photos and backup settings may sync saved content through Apple services independently of Artora.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children. The App contains no content directed at children.
