Privacy Policy — Pixora

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Overview

Pixora ("the App") is an iOS application offering an on-device AI photo toolkit: image upscaling, background removal & background composition, and photo metadata (EXIF) viewing and removal. All processing happens entirely on your device — no photos are ever uploaded to any server.

Data Collection

Pixora does not collect, store, transmit, or share your photos or any personal data.
  • All image processing is performed 100% offline using on-device AI models and native image APIs.
  • No user accounts are required.
  • No photos are uploaded to any server.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included. There are no ads.

Permissions

The App requests only the minimum access necessary:

  • Photo Library (read): Required to access a photo only after you explicitly select it via the system photo picker.
  • Photo Library (add): Required to save your edited images back to your photo library.

The App does not request camera, location, contacts, or any other sensitive permissions. The optional map used to edit a photo's GPS tag does not access your real-time location.

Purchases

Pixora offers an optional one-time in-app purchase ("Pixora Pro") that removes watermarks and unlocks high-resolution export. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. Pixora does not receive or store your payment information. Purchase validation is handled on-device via Apple's StoreKit entitlements.

Data Storage

  • Edited images are saved to your device's photo library only when you choose to save.
  • Original photos are never modified in place.
  • All AI models are bundled with the App and do not download additional data.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all App-specific data. Saved images remain in your library until you delete them.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children. The App contains no content directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
