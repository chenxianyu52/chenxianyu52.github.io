About Pixora

Pixora is an on-device AI photo toolkit for iPhone: image upscaling, background removal & background composition, and photo metadata (EXIF) viewing and removal. All processing happens entirely on your device — no photos are uploaded to any server, and no account is required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pixora work offline?

Yes. Every feature runs 100% on-device using bundled AI models. Pixora never uploads your photos and works with no network connection.

Upscaling a large photo is slow — is that normal?

Yes. High-resolution upscaling is compute-intensive and runs entirely on your device. Larger images take longer. You can keep your screen on; upscaling continues even if the screen dims or locks.

What does Pixora Pro include?

  • No watermarks on any exported image.
  • Full-resolution HD & 4K export in Upscale and Compose.
  • GPS map editing in the metadata tool.
  • A one-time purchase — not a subscription, and no ads, ever.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

I paid but Pro isn't unlocked. What should I do?

First tap Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't unlock, confirm the purchase completed in your Apple ID's purchase history, then email us at chenxianyu1012@gmail.com and we'll help.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are handled by Apple. Refund requests are managed through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my edited photos saved?

Edited images are saved to your device's Photos library only when you choose to save. Your original photos are never modified in place.

Which iOS versions are supported?

Pixora supports iOS 15.1 and later on iPhone.
